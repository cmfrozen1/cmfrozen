About Our Company

A legacy of quality and a commitment to delivering the freshest frozen vegetables from Thailand to the world.

Story

Who We Are

Chiangmai Frozen Foods Public Company Limited (CM) was established in 1988 with a vision to become a world-class manufacturer and exporter of frozen vegetables. Our primary facility is located in the heart of Thailand's agricultural region, Chiang Mai.

For over three decades, we have been at the forefront of the industry, specializing in frozen edamame, sweet corn, green beans, and baby corn. Our success is built on a foundation of quality, integrity, and long-term relationships with our farmers and customers.

Our Vision

To be the preferred global partner for high-quality frozen vegetables, recognized for excellence and sustainability.

Our Mission

We deliver the taste of nature through advanced freezing technology and strict quality standards, creating value for all stakeholders.

Our Values

Integrity, Quality, Innovation, and Social Responsibility are the core pillars that guide our every action.
